Commands

Every slash command Shockwave registers, grouped by what part of the night it handles. Parameters marked optional have a default and can be left out.

Team setup

Run these once per server, or whenever you want to reset a session.

/set-team-channels setup

Names the two voice channels teams get moved into. Creates them if they don't already exist on the server.

ParameterTypeDescription
team1stringName for the first team's voice channel.
team2stringName for the second team's voice channel.
/set-team-size setup

Sets how many players make up one side. Used when notifying people how big the game is.

ParameterTypeDescription
sizechangeintegerNumber of players per team.
/clear setup

Wipes the current teams and draft state so you can start a fresh session. Channel names, tournament data, and elo are kept unless you explicitly clear them too.

ParameterTypeDescription
clear_channelsboolean, optionalAlso forget the saved team channel names.
clear_tournamentboolean, optionalAlso clear tournament bracket data.
clear_eloboolean, optionalAlso reset stored elo/rating data.

Team formation

The three ways to actually build a roster.

/make-teams formation

Randomly splits everyone in your voice channel into two even teams. Optionally assigns a lane to each player and moves everyone automatically.

ParameterTypeDescription
use_rolesboolean, optionalAlso hand out lane roles (Top / Jungle / Mid / Bottom / Support) — needs exactly 5 players per side.
movevarboolean, optionalMove everyone into their team channel right away. On by default.

Starts a live captain draft. Name two captains, or let Shockwave pick two at random from the voice channel. Everyone else lands in a pool the captains pick from with /choose.

/choose formation

Captains only. Picks one player from the pool onto your team, then passes the turn to the other captain.

ParameterTypeDescription
membermember, optionalThe player to pick. Required unless use_random is set.
use_randomboolean, optionalPick a random remaining player instead of naming one.
Turn order is enforced. Picking out of turn, or picking as anyone other than the two captains, gets rejected with a message instead of going through.
/randomize-roles formation

Shuffles lane roles across the current teams — useful after a draft, when /make-teams wasn't run with use_roles on.

Voice channel ops

Moving people around without dragging them by hand.

/start voice

Moves the current rosters into their team channels and kicks off the game. Remembers the channel everyone started in, so /return knows where to send them back. Also opens a one-minute betting window on the match — see Betting & economy below.

/return voice

Pulls both team channels — including anyone spectating — back into the original voice channel. If the game's winner hasn't been recorded yet, any open bets are automatically refunded.

/notify voice

DMs one server member a one-time invite link to your voice channel.

ParameterTypeDescription
membermemberWho to invite.
/notify-role voice

Same as /notify, but DMs everyone holding a given role.

ParameterTypeDescription
roleroleThe role to notify.

Betting & economy

Every server member has a gold balance. Bet it on who wins the current game.

How a betting round works. Running /start opens betting for 60 seconds — the bot stays fully responsive to every other command during that window. Once it closes, Shockwave posts a message asking which team won and reacts to it with 1️⃣ and 2️⃣ itself, so reporting the result is a single click. The first valid reaction from anyone in the server records the winner and pays out bets immediately. If /return is used before a winner is reported, every bet on that round is refunded instead.
/wager betting

Bets gold on one team winning the current game. Only works while betting is open, and only one bet per player per game.

ParameterTypeDescription
amountintegerHow much gold to wager. Must not exceed your balance.
teamchoiceTeam 1 or Team 2 — whoever you think wins.
Payouts favor the underdog. Winners split the losing side's entire pool proportional to their own bet, on top of getting their wager back. A bet on the team fewer people backed pays out more than the same-sized bet on the heavily-favored side.
/daily betting

Claims 1000 free gold. Once per calendar day, per player.

/balance betting

Shows your gold balance, win/loss record, total gold wagered, and total gold won.
