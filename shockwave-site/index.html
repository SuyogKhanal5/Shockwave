Draft night, start to finish

How a session actually runs

This is the one place order genuinely matters — it's the sequence most servers follow on a real scrim night.

01

Point the bot at your voice channels

Run /set-team-channels once per server. Shockwave creates the channels if they don't exist yet.

02

Get everyone in one voice channel

Whoever runs the next command needs to be sitting in the channel with the group — that's how Shockwave knows who's playing.

03

Build the roster — casual or ranked

Shuffle instantly with /make-teams, or let two captains draft live with /captains and /choose — a casual game, elo untouched either way. Want it to count? /ranked and /ranked-captains run the exact same two paths, but balance teams by elo and update it when the winner's in. Full breakdown, side by side: casual vs. ranked setup →

04

Move everyone out, open the books

/start drops each player straight into their team's channel and opens a one-minute window for everyone to /wager gold on who wins.

05

Report the winner

Shockwave asks who won and reacts with 1️⃣/2️⃣ itself — one click settles every bet, updates game records (and elo for ranked games), and moves both teams back to the original channel automatically.

06

Handle the edge cases

/return ends a game early and refunds any bets that never got a reported winner. Clicked the wrong reaction? /report-correct-winner fixes a misreported result after the fact.
